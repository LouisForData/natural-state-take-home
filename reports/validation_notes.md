## Scientific limits

The same validation sample fits and diagnoses the bird models. Its selection design is unresolved, and clips may be dependent. Exact lower bounds and score-bin weighting are conditional diagnostics. The package does not measure recall or certify population precision. A fitted 99% probability and a one-sided lower bound reaching 99% are separate standards; the stronger bound is not a requirement stated in the brief.

Firth results are regenerated by the bird notebook and saved in `outputs/bird_firth_sensitivity.csv`. They test estimator dependence and do not change the ordinary-GLM observation policy. Firth's probability shrinkage also needs consideration before adopting it for calibration. The custom implementation is limited to an intercept and one transformed-score predictor.

Vegetation geometry tolerances and accumulation summaries prioritize review. They do not establish the cause of discrepancies or ecological adequacy. Taxonomy, form/export calculations, spatial linkage and permitted sharing of precise coordinates remain questions for the relevant team.
